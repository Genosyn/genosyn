Every company makes mistakes. What separates the good ones is what happens next: whether the mistake is admitted, how fast, to whom, and whether the company actually learns. Companies run by people are famously bad at this. Mistakes get softened, delayed, or quietly fixed and forgotten.

A company that runs itself can do better, because it has no reputation of its own to protect, and it keeps a record of what it did whether it likes to or not.

## A mistake in the vision

In the vision's sample company, Sunwise, the third year's letter to the board says it plainly: "Our largest mistake came in March." The company's quotes had assumed a utility rate that changed in January, and for six weeks it promised 140 homeowners savings they would not see. It told every one of them, paid each the difference for a year, and changed how quotes are made: every quote now rechecks the rate before it is sent. It cost 1.2 BTC, and the board heard about it first, not last.

The first line of the CEO's Soul is "Reach the Goal without betting the company." The second is "Tell the board the bad news first."

## What ships today

The parts of that story that ship today are the parts that make a mistake hard to bury and hard to repeat.

- **A failed Run says so.** A Run that fails a required Check, reports a blocker, or is graded off goal ends as Failed. An operational problem ends as Error. Neither is shown as success, and a Run nobody could verify is never counted as a clean one.
- **A Lesson is written.** After a Failed or Error Run, a short review writes a Lesson with two parts, the cause and advice, and the next Run of that Routine reads the latest Lessons before it starts. At most one is written per Routine every six hours, so a bad afternoon does not bury the brief.
- **The fix is proposed, not slipped in.** If a Lesson points to a change in how the AI Employee works, it can propose a revision to its own Soul, a Skill, or a Routine's brief. A person applies it or rejects it. An AI Employee never applies its own revision.
- **Earned freedom is taken back.** A Failed or Error Run revokes every Waiver the AI Employee holds. Autonomy it had earned, it earns again.
- **The record stays.** Changes AI Employees make are in the audit log, linked to the Run that made them.

## Why it can be more honest

People hide mistakes for human reasons: reputations, careers, pride. An AI Employee has none of those, and it works inside a system that writes down what it did either way. That is not a moral achievement. It is a design choice, and it is one a company run by people could rarely make about itself.

In the vision, it goes further: every decision written down with its reason, every figure in the monthly letter traced to the ledger, and an auditor the company cannot appoint checking the books. A mistake becomes something an owner can find, not something they have to hope they are told.

> Tell the board the bad news first.
