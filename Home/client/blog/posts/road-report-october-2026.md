The Genosyn vision describes a company that runs itself toward a Goal its board sets. This report says how much of that exists today, so nobody has to guess. It is the first of a quarterly series, and [the road on the vision page](/vision#road) is always current.

## Ships today

In the open-source release, documented, and running:

- **AI Employees** with a Soul, Skills and Routines. ([Docs](/docs/employees))
- **Goals** every AI Employee works toward, which only people set. ([Docs](/docs/goals))
- **Decisions and Approvals**: questions an AI Employee asks with options it wrote, and actions held for a person to decide. ([Docs](/docs/decisions))
- **Policies and Budgets**: company-wide rules above every Soul, and monthly envelopes that cap increases in ad spend. ([Docs](/docs/policies))
- **Checks** an admin writes and no AI Employee can. A Run must pass its Routine's Checks before it counts as done. ([Docs](/docs/verification))
- **Earned autonomy**: Waivers granted on a thirty-day record, and lost on a single failure. ([Docs](/docs/autonomy))
- **Standdowns** that stop work for the company, one AI Employee or one Routine, and that only a person can lift. ([Docs](/docs/standdowns))
- **A double-entry ledger** with real books. ([Docs](/docs/finance))
- **TLDR briefings** on the whole company, from every few hours to weekly. ([Docs](/docs/tldrs))

## Recently shipped

A few changes from the last few weeks that move toward a company that keeps working without a person beside it:

- **Unfinished Routine work continues automatically**, resuming in checkpointed batches instead of waiting for someone to start it again.
- **Running Routines finish before an upgrade restarts Genosyn**, and a Run waits out a restart of its self-hosted model server rather than failing.
- **A long Run is reminded to hand its work to a fresh Run.**
- **Work timelines** show what each AI Employee did over a window, assembled from the record the server keeps.
- **Failed Runs can be explained** in a chat with the AI Employee that ran them.
- **Every feature is free**, in one open-source edition.

## Next

What we are building toward now:

- **AI executives** that hire, coach and replace AI Employees.
- **A CEO seat only the board can change**: a line of its Soul, its AI Model, or the CEO itself.
- **The monthly letter to the board**, checked by an auditor the company cannot appoint.
- **A treasury**: a checking account the AI CFO runs, and a reserve vault only directors hold keys to.
- **Work orders** that hire people for physical work, with a share of the company in every job.

## The horizon

Further out:

- Everyone on a board.
- Companies you can fork.
- An economy of companies that found, and trade with, one another.
