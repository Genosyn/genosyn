On the vision page, the board's first act is to write one sentence, and everything the company does afterwards follows from it. So the sentence is worth getting right, and it is harder than it looks.

## What a Goal is

A Goal is a measurable objective: a direction and a number. In Genosyn today, a Goal has a title, a description, and a target with a direction: reach a number, for things that should go up, or drive down to one, for things that should come down. It can also have a deadline, an AI Employee accountable for it, and a parent Goal it rolls up into. Its progress can be reported by hand or read from a chart.

Only owners and admins create or change Goals. Every AI Employee works with them in front of it: its own active Goals and the company's top-level Goals are part of every prompt it gets. AI Employees report progress. They never write a Goal.

## Start with the direction

A good Goal says what the company is for in words a stranger would understand. "Put solar on a million roofs" is a direction. "Grow" is not. If an AI Employee reading the Goal could not tell which of two choices serves it better, the direction is too vague.

## Then the number

A number makes a Goal checkable. Sunwise's is a million roofs, with revenue growing 40% a year. The number does three jobs: it tells the company how far it has to go, it tells the board whether it is on track, and one day it settles the question. In Genosyn, a Goal settles automatically, once, as achieved or missed, and its owners are told.

## Then the line it must not cross

Every number can be hit in ways nobody wanted. It is the oldest problem with targets: once a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure. Revenue can grow by overcharging. Roofs can go up badly. So a Goal needs company, and Genosyn gives it some:

- **Link Routines to the Goal.** Work that meets its own criteria but works against the Goal is graded off goal, and the Run counts as Failed.
- **Write the lines in a Policy.** A Policy sits above every AI Employee's Soul in every prompt, and can block tools and email domains outright, for people and AI Employees alike.
- **Put the constraint in the sentence when it matters most.** Sunwise's CEO's Soul opens with "Reach the Goal without betting the company."

## Weak and better

| Weak | Better |
| --- | --- |
| Grow the business | Reach $2M in annual recurring revenue by December, with churn under 3% a month |
| Improve support | Drive median first-response time down to 10 minutes, without closing a conversation unanswered |
| Do good for the town | Clean water for every village in the district, with every pump working |

## How a Goal should change

Rarely, and only on purpose. A Goal that changes every month is not a Goal; it is a mood. In the vision, only the board can change the Goal, and the company raises it only when the Goal is met or the world has moved: "We met the Goal. What should the next one be?" The company writes the options. The board chooses.

> Running a company is no longer the hard part. Choosing its Goal is.
